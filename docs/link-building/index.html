Link Building & Off-Page Reputation

Updated 2026-08-05 · by Connor Enge

On this page

  • Why links matter
  • How to earn links the durable way
  • Anchor text & internal links
  • What to avoid

Links from other sites are votes of confidence. They remain one of Google's strongest signals — but only when they're earned, not manufactured.

Why links matter

When a reputable site links to yours, it passes trust and helps Google discover and contextualize your content. Relevance and authority of the linking site matter far more than raw count.

How to earn links the durable way

  • Publish genuinely link-worthy assets: original research, data, tools, definitive guides.
  • Digital PR — give journalists and creators something worth citing.
  • Relationships — guest contributions, expert quotes, partnerships in your niche.
  • Reclaim unlinked mentions of your brand and fix broken links pointing at you.

Anchor text & internal links

Natural, descriptive anchor text helps; over-optimized exact-match anchors at scale look manipulative. Don't neglect internal links — they're the links you fully control and they distribute authority across your own pages.

What to avoid

Google's spam policies target link schemes: buying/selling links that pass ranking credit, large-scale reciprocal or exchange schemes, and automated link generation. These risk manual actions or algorithmic suppression. If a tactic exists only to trick the ranking system, treat it as a liability.

The most durable link strategy is being the resource other people want to cite.
