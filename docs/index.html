The Complete Guide to SEO

Updated 2026-08-05 · by Connor Enge

On this page

  • What Google actually rewards
  • The four pillars
  • How to use this guide

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the practice of helping search engines understand your content and helping people find it. Done right, it is not a bag of tricks — it is the discipline of publishing genuinely useful pages and removing every technical obstacle between them and a searcher.

What Google actually rewards

Google's ranking systems are built to surface helpful, reliable, people-first content — not content made to game rankings. Everything in this guide ladders up to three questions Google is effectively asking on every query:

  • Can I find and read this page? (crawling, indexing, performance)
  • Is it the most helpful, trustworthy answer to the query? (content, E-E-A-T)
  • Do other people treat it as a credible source? (links, reputation)

The four pillars

  1. Keyword research — understand the exact intent behind what people search.
  2. On-page SEO — make each page unmistakably about its topic.
  3. Technical SEO — be fast, crawlable, mobile-friendly, and structured.
  4. Link building & reputation — earn signals that you are a trusted source.

Underneath all of it sits content quality and E-E-A-T, and around all of it sits measurement — because SEO is a loop, not a launch.

How to use this guide

Read it in order the first time, then treat it as a checklist. Start with how search works so every later tactic has a reason behind it.

There are no secrets that automatically rank a site first. Following the fundamentals just makes it far easier for search engines to crawl, understand, and trust your content.
